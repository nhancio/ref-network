# Ref Network brand kit

**A referral, ready at checkout.**

Ref Network gives local businesses an offer people can share. The shopper sees the discount at checkout, and the person who made the introduction can earn points after a paid purchase.

Use the horizontal logo at 145 px wide or larger. Leave at least one end dot’s width clear around the visible artwork. Use the standalone mark or app icon when space is tight. The SVG artwork includes its own lettering outlines, so the logo works without installed fonts.

**Typography** uses Instrument Serif for large headlines, Bricolage Grotesque for product headings, and DM Sans for body text and controls. Keep rupee amounts and terms readable in product screens. [Instrument Serif](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif) · [Bricolage Grotesque](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bricolage+Grotesque) · [DM Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans)

## Why this direction

- **The path** connects the shopper, the referrer and the business. It ends like a check mark so the same symbol can suggest the checkout moment.
- **The counter stamp** was a second direction. A small receipt works well in marketing because it shows the exact savings. The core mark stays useful in the rewards view and across business categories.
- **The network map** can become an illustration for finding introductions. Start the brand with one visible referral path so a first-time shopper can understand it quickly.

The visual identity builds on the existing landing page’s ivory, terracotta and olive. The campaign image reuses the generated neighbourhood shop scene from the prototype. The people and bill shown in the materials are illustrative.
