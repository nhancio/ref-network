# Ref Network launch copy

## Positioning

**Main line**  
A referral, ready at checkout.

**One sentence**  
Ref Network gives local businesses a way to share offers through people they trust, apply the discount at checkout, and reward referrals that lead to paid sales.

**Social bio**  
Referral offers for local businesses. Share a link, get the offer at checkout, and earn points on completed referrals.

**Landing page lead**  
Turn “I know the owner” into a discount.

**For a merchant**  
Give each referrer a link. Set the offer, then see which referrals lead to paid sales.

**For a shopper**  
Open the offer link before you buy. Your savings appear when you open the shop’s bill in the same browser and meet the offer terms.

**For a referrer**  
Earn points when someone you refer makes a qualifying purchase. See all your balances together and use each one at the business that awarded it.

## Founder post

When a friend sends you to a shop, the recommendation often comes through WhatsApp. The shop still has to work out which offer you’re eligible for at the counter.

I’m testing Ref Network with local businesses. A shop gives someone an offer link to share. The shopper sees the discount on their bill, and the person who referred them can earn points after the purchase.

We’re starting with shops, cafés and service businesses in one neighbourhood. If you run one and would try a small referral campaign, reply **SHOP** and tell me your neighbourhood. If you’d try an offer from a friend, reply **TRY**.

## WhatsApp invite for a merchant

Hi [name], I’m testing Ref Network with a few businesses in [neighbourhood]. You set an offer and give regular customers their own link to share. The discount comes through on the bill, and you can see which introductions led to sales.

Would you be up for a 10-minute demo? We could try it with one offer and a small limit to see how people use it.

## WhatsApp message from a referrer

Sharing an offer from [business name]. It’s [offer] on [minimum spend or eligible items], valid through [date]. Open [offer link] before you go, then open the shop’s bill link in the same browser to see the discount.

[If applicable] I earn points with this business if you make a qualifying purchase.

## A quick way to test interest

1. **Choose one neighbourhood.** Start wherever you can personally reach shop owners and people who recommend them. Indiranagar is an example, and the first venue should come from your own network.
2. **Try two opening messages.** Lead with **“Bring in more customers through friends”** for half the merchants and **“Give referred customers a clear offer at checkout”** for the other half. Use the same demo and ask.
3. **Measure what people do.** Record replies, demos, campaigns started, links shared, paid referrals, and owners who want to run it again. Aim for 3 campaign setups from the first 10 qualified merchant conversations as an initial test target.
4. **Learn why people return.** Ask referrers whether the points make them more likely to share again, and ask merchants what they would pay for a verified referred purchase. Compare that with the discount and reward they chose to fund.

Use the launch graphic with the founder post and the link preview when the pilot page is hosted. The first campaign should name the issuing business and show the exact discount, terms and points available.
