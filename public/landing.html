How it works

From a shared link to a settled bill.

Asha has a friend who runs a gift shop. Here’s how she can pass on an offer to someone in her family group.

  1. 01

    The shop sets the offer

    The owner sets 10% off, a ₹700 minimum spend, an end date and a limit on how many shoppers can use it.

  2. 02

    Asha shares her link

    The owner gives Asha her own referral link. Asha shares it in the family WhatsApp group.

  3. 03

    Meera opens the offer

    Meera sees the terms on her phone. Ref remembers the offer in her browser. The shop can choose to review requests first.

  4. 04

    Ref applies the discount

    Meera opens the shop’s bill link and sees her savings automatically. Once staff mark it paid, Ref adds any earned points to Asha’s account.

Built for how people refer in India

Put the offer in the conversation people already have.

A family WhatsApp group, an apartment community, or a regular recommending a favourite café can be the starting point. A referral link carries the offer terms from that conversation to the counter.

For businesses
Give trusted contacts a trackable link. Control the discount and offer limit, then see which links lead to completed sales.
For customers
See the terms before making the trip. Open the bill link in the same browser to see the discount and pay the shop the usual way.
For referrers
Earn points from participating businesses when your referrals make paid purchases. See them together and use them at the business that awarded them.

FAQ

What to know before you share.

Who can claim an offer?

A reusable link can qualify its holders while the offer is available. A single-use invite lets one shopper use it. The merchant chooses whether to approve offers automatically or review each request first.

Does Ref contact my friends or read my address book?

You choose who gets a link and send it in WhatsApp or any channel you already use. The current demo qualifies shoppers by referral link. Verified phone-number or LinkedIn matching would require a future integration.

How does it fit into billing?

Staff enter the bill reference and subtotal once. The shopper opens the bill link, confirms the discounted total, and pays the business directly. A store or point-of-sale integration can pass in the bill details for the live version.

When does a referral reward count?

Ref credits points to the direct referrer after staff record a qualifying paid purchase. One point gives ₹1 off at the business that awarded it. Your rewards page shows a balance for each business.

Can businesses limit how many offers go out?

Yes. Merchants can set an offer limit and end date, pause the campaign or revoke a link. Approved offers can be used through the end date if the purchase meets the terms.
