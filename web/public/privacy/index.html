Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Operated by Nhancio (nithindidigam@nhancio.com)

Core commitment: Your imported contacts belong strictly to your private account. Ref Network never sells contact lists, never emails or messages your contacts without your action, and never exposes raw contact details (emails, phone numbers, or personal notes) to other users.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: Name, email address, and profile photo provided when you sign in via Google Sign-In or email and password (Firebase Authentication).
  • Contacts you explicitly import: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, organizations, job titles, profile URLs, and optional notes imported when you connect Google Contacts, upload a LinkedIn Connections.csv, upload a .vcf/.csv file, or add a contact manually.
  • Circle connections & intro requests: Mutual circle links you create via one-time invitations, high-level company reach counts you choose to share with your circle, and private warm introduction requests you send or receive.

2. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

When you choose to connect Google Contacts, Ref Network requests read-only access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts.readonly) solely to import your contacts into your private relationship graph so you can search organizations and trace warm introduction paths.

Ref Network's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only access Google Contacts when you explicitly trigger a sync.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, data brokerage, or training third-party AI/ML models.
  • We do not share your Google Contacts records with third parties or other Ref Network users.

3. How Data Is Stored & Protected

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (region asia-south1) with per-user Firestore Security Rules enforcing that documents under users/{uid}/contacts can only be read or written by the authenticated owner (request.auth.uid == uid). Connections with friends in your Ref Circle share only aggregated organization names and counts, never individual contact emails or phone numbers.

4. Your Controls & Data Deletion

  • You can edit or delete any individual contact directly in your Ref Network workspace.
  • You can remove any Ref Circle connection at any time.
  • You can revoke Ref Network's Google Contacts permission anytime from your Google Account Permissions page.
  • To request full account and data deletion, email nithindidigam@nhancio.com from your registered email address and we will permanently delete your records within 7 days.

5. Contact

Questions about privacy or data handling? Email nithindidigam@nhancio.com.
